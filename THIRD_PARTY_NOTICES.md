# Third-party notices

Redmond Calculator consumes third-party projects without changing their
ownership or license terms.

## Microsoft Calculator

Source: <https://github.com/microsoft/calculator>

The pinned source, MIT license, and Microsoft third-party notices are contained
in:

- `upstream/windows-calculator/LICENSE`
- `upstream/windows-calculator/NOTICE.txt`

Distributions that include Microsoft Calculator source, resources, fonts, or
compiled engine code must include the applicable copyright, license, and notice
material.

## Managed and UI dependencies

The project also consumes Avalonia, FluentAvalonia, CommunityToolkit.Mvvm,
AngouriMath, and their transitive dependencies through their package managers.
Their package metadata and license files remain authoritative.
